# Changelog

## v3.0.0
- SEQ page rebuilt as a lane editor: note, sound, word, gate, velocity, probability and
  components are edited right on the step; E3 on an empty step creates it
- word lane: dial the vocabulary directly onto a step
- OP-XY-style multi-step selection on the grid step page
- syllable keyboard grid page (onset + vowel + coda, automatic SpeakJet variants)
- vocabulary grown to 5000 words, browsable by letter or by 16 themes; 64 ready-made phrases;
  about 550 pronunciation exceptions
- word gap setting for spreading phrases across steps
- stutter / beat repeat on the master bus
- splash screen, scope, formant spectrum curve, redesigned sequencer view

## v2.0.0
- 2 x 64-step sequencer with per-step pitch, gate, velocity, probability
- 16 OP-XY-style step components
- directions, swing, triplet divisions, transpose
- vocabulary page (3000 words) and improved text-to-speech
- own effects bus: tape delay, FDN reverb with shimmer, chorus, saturation, wavefolder,
  bitcrusher, ring mod, comb robot
- animated mouth UI

## v1.0.0
- SpeakJet 5-channel synth emulation in SuperCollider, two voices
- 72 allophones, 55 sound effects, SpeakJet control codes
- English text-to-speech, grid pages, 16-step sequencer with tremolo and babble
